2nd ChatApp

A second WhatsApp Web account on your iPhone or iPad.

Getting started

  1. Open 2nd ChatApp. A QR code appears.
  2. On the phone with your other account, open WhatsApp → Settings → Linked Devices → Link a Device.
  3. Scan the QR code. Your second account is now available in 2nd ChatApp.

Free vs. Premium

The free version signs you out every 7 days; you can link again by scanning the QR code. Premium keeps you signed in. WhatsApp itself may still sign out linked devices, for example after a long period without using your phone.

Subscription

Premium is a monthly auto-renewing subscription billed through your Apple ID. Manage or cancel it in Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions. On a new device, tap Restore Purchases on the upgrade screen. Refunds are handled by Apple via reportaproblem.apple.com.

Troubleshooting

  • QR code doesn’t appear: check your internet connection and tap Retry.
  • Voice messages don’t record: allow microphone access in Settings → 2nd ChatApp.
  • Links open in Safari: this is intentional, for your safety.
