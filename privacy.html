Privacy Policy

2nd ChatApp for iPhone and iPad · Last updated: 11 October 2026

In short: 2nd ChatApp does not collect, store or share any personal data on our side. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no servers of our own. Everything the app keeps stays on your device.

Who is responsible

2nd ChatApp is developed by Milan Dima, Belgium (the “developer”, “we”). For any privacy question or request, contact 2ndchatapp@gmail.com.

What the app is

2nd ChatApp shows WhatsApp Web (web.whatsapp.com) in a separate app, so you can use a second account on the same device. 2nd ChatApp is an independent app and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by WhatsApp or Meta Platforms, Inc.

Your messages and WhatsApp data

When you link an account, the app loads WhatsApp Web directly from WhatsApp’s servers, just as Safari would. Your messages, contacts and media travel between your device and WhatsApp only. The app does not read, copy, modify or transmit that content, and we never receive it. WhatsApp’s own Privacy Policy applies to your use of WhatsApp Web.

The WhatsApp Web session (cookies and website storage) is saved on your device by Apple’s WebKit so you stay signed in. In the free version, this session data is deleted from your device every 7 days, which signs you out. Deleting the app removes all of it.

Data stored on your device

Besides the WhatsApp Web session, the app stores three small settings on your device only:

  • whether you have finished the welcome screen;
  • whether your subscription is active (a cached copy of what Apple reports);
  • the date of the last automatic sign-out (free version).

These never leave your device and are removed when you delete the app.

Purchases

Subscriptions are sold and processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your name, Apple ID, or payment details. Apple only tells the app whether a subscription is active. Apple’s Privacy Policy applies to purchases.

Camera and microphone

The app asks for camera or microphone access only when you use a feature of WhatsApp Web that needs it, such as recording a voice message. Access is granted only to web.whatsapp.com, is handled by iOS, and can be withdrawn at any time in Settings. We do not record or store any audio or video.

Links

Links you open from chats are opened in your default browser, not inside the app.

Tracking

The app does not track you across apps or websites, contains no third-party analytics or advertising SDKs, and does not use your data for advertising.

Children

The app is not directed at children. WhatsApp’s minimum age requirements apply to the use of WhatsApp.

Your rights (GDPR)

Because we do not process personal data on our side, there is normally nothing for us to access, correct or delete; all app data is on your device and is deleted with the app. You can still contact us with any question at the address above. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Belgian Data Protection Authority (Gegevensbeschermingsautoriteit / Autorité de protection des données).

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.
